# Code of Conduct

## Standard

Contributors must communicate professionally, review evidence rather than people,
and keep technical disagreement focused on observable behavior, contracts, risks,
and tradeoffs.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct without being abusive.
- Separate confirmed facts from inference.
- Disclose conflicts of interest that affect technical decisions.
- Respect security, confidentiality, and data-classification boundaries.
- Give actionable review feedback with file, contract, or test references.
- Correct mistakes openly when new evidence changes the conclusion.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of secrets,
malicious code, fabricated validation, and retaliation are prohibited.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation,
or escalate incidents through the Quantum L9 organization. Security-sensitive
reports must follow `SECURITY.md` rather than public issue discussion.
